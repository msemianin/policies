Data Deletion Instructions

To request the deletion of your player data (scores, leaderboards, and analytics), please follow these steps to ensure we can identify your account:

Please provide the following information in your first email:

  1. Your Google Play Games Profile Name (to remove your record from Unity Leaderboards).
  2. Your Advertising ID (to remove your data from Facebook and Tenjin analytics).
    Found in: Settings -> Google -> Ads

How to send the request:

  • Email to: msq2contact@gmail.com
  • Subject line: [REMOVE DATA]

Upon receiving these details, we will manually remove your entries from our Unity Gaming Services Leaderboards and trigger data deletion in Facebook and Tenjin within 30 days.
